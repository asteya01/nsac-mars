    Terrain = NASA data

    This ground is the elevation grid of a real Mars region, exported from NASA Mars Trek as a 3D terrain file. The shape of the land comes straight from that file; no noise is added to it.

    Live under your boots

    Proof: the SHA-256 above is the hash of the exported file, and verify_nasa_dem.py re-reads that file and compares it with this grid. Honest notes: the export has no ground scale, so one grid unit is treated as one metre; heights are stretched 2x so craters and ridges read clearly; the outer skirt row of the export is removed; flat pads for the base buildings, the ice spring and the crash gouge are game edits.

    What is real and what is invented here.

    Real: the constants in Facts, the Mars clock and sun angle, the landing site and rover names, and the images in Photos.

    Modelled: temperature through the day, radiation inside shelters, oxygen and battery use. These follow published mission numbers loosely and are tuned for play.

    Invented: the terrain layout, the greenhouse and the base layout. Everything else follows real Mars physics: 3.71 m/s2 gravity everywhere, no liquid water at the surface, and no open fire in the thin carbon dioxide air.
